FAQ

Add-On Services questions

How do I add extras to my booking? add

Select them in the Extras step of the online booking form. You can also log in to your account and add them to an upcoming visit.

Can I add something on the day of the cleaning? add

Often, yes. Ask your cleaner and if time allows we will add it and update your invoice.

Are add-ons included in deep cleaning? add

Deep Cleaning covers detailed surface work. Inside the oven, inside the fridge and interior windows remain optional add-ons so you only pay for what you need.

Do you offer carpet shampooing? add

Not at this time. We vacuum thoroughly and can recommend a trusted local carpet cleaner for stains and extraction.
