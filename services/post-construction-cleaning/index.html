Post-Construction Cleaning

Renovations leave behind a fine layer of dust on everything. We remove it from every vent, cabinet and corner so your new space is ready to enjoy or show.

Book Online arrow_forward Ask a Question

Overview

Post-construction cleaning is a specialized service. Drywall dust, paint overspray, adhesive residue and sticker marks require different tools and more time than a standard clean. Our team works methodically from the top down so dust is not simply pushed around.

We serve homeowners finishing a remodel, contractors preparing for a final walk-through, and builders and investors getting a property ready to list. Rough, final and touch-up phases are all available.

FAQ

Post-Construction Cleaning questions

When should post-construction cleaning happen? add

After the last trade has finished and all debris is removed. For large projects we can do a rough clean mid-project and a final clean at completion.

Do you remove construction debris? add

We bag and remove small debris and dust. Large materials, lumber and drywall scraps should be removed by your contractor before we arrive.

How is it priced? add

Post-construction pricing is based on square footage and the condition of the space. Email us with details or photos for a quote.

Can you clean a single remodeled room? add

Yes. Kitchen and bathroom remodels are among our most common post-construction jobs.
