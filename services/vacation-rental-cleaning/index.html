Vacation Rental Cleaning

Las Vegas hosts live and die by reviews. Our turnover service keeps your Airbnb, VRBO or corporate rental guest-ready every time, with photo confirmation and restocking built in.

Book Online arrow_forward Ask a Question

Overview

Turnover cleaning is more than a quick tidy. We strip and remake beds, launder or swap linens, sanitize every surface, restage the space to your photos and restock essentials so your next guest walks into a hotel-quality experience.

We sync with your booking calendar so cleanings are scheduled automatically between check-out and check-in. Each visit ends with a damage and inventory check and photos sent to you, so you always know the condition of your property.

FAQ

Vacation Rental Cleaning questions

Can you handle same-day turnovers? add

Yes. Same-day turnovers between check-out and check-in are our specialty. We confirm timing from your calendar.

Do you provide linens and supplies? add

We can launder your linens on site or manage a swap set. Restocking of consumables is available with items billed at cost.

What if a guest leaves damage? add

Our team documents any damage with photos and notifies you immediately so you can file a claim before the next guest arrives.

Do you work with property managers? add

Yes. We support individual hosts as well as property managers with multiple units across the valley.
