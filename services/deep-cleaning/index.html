Deep Cleaning

Dust, grime and hard-water buildup collect fast in the desert. Our Deep Cleaning resets your home from ceiling to baseboard so every surface looks and feels new.

Book Online arrow_forward Ask a Question

Overview

Deep Cleaning includes everything in our Standard Cleaning plus the detail work that is easy to put off: baseboards, door frames, light fixtures, ceiling fans, vents, blinds, cabinet fronts and the buildup behind and under furniture that we can safely move.

It is ideal before hosting, after a long stretch without professional help, for seasonal resets, or as the first visit before starting a recurring plan. Many Las Vegas homeowners book one each spring and fall to stay ahead of the dust.

FAQ

Deep Cleaning questions

How often should I book a deep cleaning? add

Most homes benefit from a deep cleaning two to four times per year, with standard or recurring cleanings in between.

How long does it take? add

Plan for four to eight hours depending on the size and condition of the home. Larger homes may be scheduled with two cleaners.

Is inside the oven or fridge included? add

Those are available as add-ons so you only pay for what you need. You can select them when you book online.

Can you move furniture? add

We move light furniture such as chairs, side tables and small rugs. For safety we do not move heavy items like beds, dressers or appliances.
