Overview

Choose weekly, bi-weekly or monthly visits and we handle the rest. Each visit follows our Standard Cleaning checklist, and we rotate in extra detail tasks so your home never slips back to needing a deep clean.

Recurring plans are flexible. Skip a visit when you travel, add an extra clean before guests arrive, or change your frequency any time from your online account. There are no contracts and no cancellation fees with 24 hours notice.

FAQ

Recurring Cleaning questions

Which frequency is right for me? add

Weekly works well for busy families and homes with pets. Bi-weekly is our most popular plan. Monthly suits smaller households or homes that stay tidy between visits.

Do I need a deep cleaning first? add

If your home has not been professionally cleaned in the last month or two, we recommend starting with a Deep Cleaning so your recurring visits can focus on maintenance.

Can I skip a visit? add

Yes. Skip or reschedule from your online account with at least 24 hours notice at no charge.

Will I have the same cleaner each time? add

We do our best to send the same cleaner or team to every visit. If they are unavailable, we send another trained team member with your home notes.
