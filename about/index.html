About Us

Locally owned. Detail obsessed.

Aven Cleaning Co. is a Las Vegas cleaning company built around one goal: making life easier for the people and businesses we serve.

Our story

Aven Cleaning Co. was founded by Kasey Rivera right here in Las Vegas. After seeing how many cleaning services over-promised and under-delivered, Kasey set out to build a company that busy homeowners, families, professionals and business owners could rely on without a second thought.

Our approach is simple. Hire great people and background-check every one of them. Use professional products and a detailed checklist on every visit. Price everything upfront so there are no surprises. And make booking as easy as ordering dinner.

Today we serve homes, short-term rentals and commercial spaces across the Las Vegas valley, and most of our clients come to us through referrals from neighbors and friends. We think that is the best review we could ask for.

Kasey Rivera

Owner, Aven Cleaning Co.
