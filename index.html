About Aven Cleaning Co.

Cleaning you can count on, from people you can trust

Aven Cleaning Co. was founded in Las Vegas by Kasey Rivera with a simple idea: a cleaning company should make life easier, not add to the to-do list. That means showing up on time, doing exactly what we said we would, and leaving your home or workplace noticeably better than we found it.

We serve busy homeowners, families, professionals, short-term rental hosts and local businesses who value convenience and want a dependable team they can use again and again.
